# Time Pigmy (Ludum Dare 19)

Time Pygmy was developed for the Ludum Dare 19 competition. The theme was “discovery“.

You are a pygmy from the paleolithic period and you accidentaly open a breach through time to modern days. This is a good opportunity to discover things and bring back modern concepts to your time !

This is my first Ludum Dare entry and the first complete game I created outside of Motion Twin for 10 years (don’t get me wrong, I love my company). And it was pretty much like a revelation: I could actually have a game idea, develop it and finish it within 48h! This felt f*** awesome.
